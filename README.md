# Planet-Prismaris
A Factorio mod where you visit the ever-cycling world of Prismaris, a world of infinite but constantly-shifting resources. This mod is currently very early in its development process.

## Overview
*Prismaris* is a planet of ever-shifting resources. Everything is constantly becoming something else all the time through the spoilage mechanic, but nothing is ever lost for good. Almost every recipe has multiple possible outputs and many items spoil after a short duration into another item in a cycle. Recycling is also borked on this planet; the only way to stem the tide is to embrace the sushi and pray nothing clogs.

Prismaris is *intended* to be done between the inner planets and aquilo, but strictly speaking it only requires Gleba first. It eligible for any planet start.

Prismaris's terrain is based loosely on the generation of aquilo, with small islands of solid ground and vast seas of entropy.

## Resources
Prismaris comes with a few specific resources, each with a unique gimmick to processing them:
- Prismatic Shards - Can be split into items with an a fast-shifting RGBW or CMYK cycle. Opposing pairs can be converted into iron ore, copper ore, or stone. White and Black variants are less useful and hog belt space, having the longest spoil times. Prismatic Shard patches are small and far apart, but are literally infinite.
- Aethric Shards - Can be "shattered" into a set of (seemingly) identical variants, then further "destabilized" into forms that decay into solid petrochemical resources (coal, carbon, solid fuel), organic resources (jelly & yumako mash) and calcite. Aethric shards are like plants using an agricultural tower. They have a very fast growth time, but relatively low yield per harvest. One of the variants of the shattered aethric shard (which has the longest spoil time and the only unique icon of the set) acts as a seed for the crystals.
- Entropic Sea - A fluid resource from Prismaris's ocean. Can be separated into entropic dust and water. Entropic dust rapidly flickers between a positive and negative form; only the positive form is useful, and every process that uses it produces the negative form as a byproduct. It's also very hard to get rid of entropic dust as you generally consume less than you produce and it can't be recycled away. Positive Entropic Dust also has a small fuel value... and a burn result of negative entropic dust, meaning you can't actually get rid of it by burning it. It can also be converted into entropic nutrients, but those spoil/burn back to negative entropic dust too. Productivity bonuses can quickly cause this to grow out of control. On Prismaris, excess can be tossed back into the lava-like ocean. On other planets, it can be voided by tossing it into lava or launched into space; entropic dust has a *very* high rocket capacity for this reason.
- Void Essence - A pumped fluid. Useful in many processes, particularly as a catalyst. Like Fluoroketone, comes in multiple variants that must be cycled between, but tends to be ever-so-slightly "lossy" at first. Sufficient (and *easily* attainable) levels of productivity can make the process net positive, however.

## Environment
Prismaris has a few distinct environmental features:
- Lightning - Like Fulgora, Prismaris has lightning storms, though these go all day and night (stronger during the day). These lightning strikes are far less frequent than on Fulgora and each bolt has less energy, so the power they provide is significantly diminished but they still necessitate protection.
- Thin Air - Like Aquilo, the air in Prismaris is thin, increasing the energy usage of robots significantly, though not as much as Aquilo.
- Low solar and long nights - The solar power on Prismaris is fairly low, while the night time is incredibly long.
- Tainted Water - The only source of water on the planet has a physical byproduct that must be dealt with
- Broken Recycling - Fulgora's recycler won't accept any item native to Prismaris


## Science Pack:
Prismaris has the "Polyphasic Science Pack," which comes in three forms that cycle on a timer by using the spoilage mechanic, similar to Gleba's agricultural science pack. Basic technologies need one of the three variants (Red / Green / Blue) while more advanced technologies might need two or even three different packs. Any pack that spoils becomes the next pack in the cycle.

## Rewards
Prismaris offers several unique rewards.

### Exclusive Buildings
- Crystal Separator - Furnace prototype for processing prismatic and Aethric shards. Happily accepts any variant put into it and spits out *something,* though sometimes that something is junk.


### Alternate Recipes
- Provides an alternate recipe for unlocking/crafting the agricultural tower and the biochamber, the latter of which does not include pentapod eggs.


### Technology Rewards:
- Chronocycler - A unique "Multi-phase building," comes in four variants. Uses Void Essence as a liquid fuel. All work similar to Fulgora's recycler, but with a twist:
	- Unconfigured Chronocycler - The base item. It cannot be placed as a building and instead serves as an intermediate part for all other chronocyclers. All other chronocyclers eventually spoil back into this form after one hour.
	- Reversal Chronocycler - Works like a normal recycler, but has an innate quality and productivity bonus at the cost of being slightly slower. It is also capable of recycling Prismaris's unique items.
	- Stasis Chronocycler - Intended for preservation of perishable items. Depending on your mod settings, this either resets the spoil percentage of a perishable item, or it transforms a perishable item into a version with a much longer spoil time that must be transformed back to be used.
	- Acceleration Chronocycler - Takes in an item with a spoil time, and immediately produces its spoil result. There is a small chance an item is lost but also a small innate chance of increasing the item's quality.
- Lunar Panels - Inverted, more efficient solar panels that only work at night rather than during the day.
- Cogitor - Uses a complex crystal catalyst to work. The catalyst very slowly recharges itself after being expended, and isn't consumed. Can transmute science packs in a lossy cycle, but has a very high innate quality bonus. Effectively, this lets you sacrifice a small amount of your science packs to gain a significant boost in their per-pack value.
- Prismatic Laser & Tesla Turrets - Laser and Tesla turrets that do damage types other than "laser" or "electric." Uses the same "Unconfigured" / 1-hour spoil-reversion mechanic of the chronocycler, though it takes a specific prismatic shard to configure and that shard is lost if it reverts. Beam matches the color of the prismatic shard. Damage types include Fire, Acid, Electric (for lasers) / Laser (for tesla), Poison, Physical, and Explosion.
- Void Beacons - Beacons that allow productivity/quality modules and have both a high multiplier and high transmission range, but use concentrated void fluid as their fuel instead of electricity, outputting impure void essence as waste product.
- Additional Lab Speed + 3 (non-infinite) levels of research productivity
- 2 levels of worker (non-infinite) robot speed
